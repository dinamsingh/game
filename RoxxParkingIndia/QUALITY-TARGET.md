# Current quality target

The user's acceptance target is the supplied original/reference game's visual finish and course-by-course challenge. Version 0.3.0 does not establish that target. Its extra districts, smaller bays, route gates, medals and passing automated tests demonstrate functionality rather than visual or handling parity.

The evidence-based gap analysis is [COMPARISON.md](../apk-analysis/comparison/COMPARISON.md); authored-layout diagrams and the original static Unity study are linked there. Reference APK textures were decoded only under `apk-analysis/comparison/reference-previews/` for analysis and were not imported into this project.

Next implementation milestone is one finished loading-yard/workshop course using original/licensed meshes with coherent scale, UV/material detail, believable environment composition, a stable normal driving view and two constrained manoeuvres plus a reverse setup. Establish that visual/gameplay bar before expanding or reskinning the rest of the courses. Preserve working controls, camera, imported Thar rig, Android compatibility and saved progress.

0.4.0 implementation advances that milestone with a rebuilt level 05 service yard, asymmetric workshop architecture, CC0 normal/AO-mapped industrial props and an always-accessible practice entry. Review its actual driving and frontage captures before accepting the art. Full human driving, material/vehicle finish and difficulty calibration remain acceptance work; this update is not a claim that the reference quality bar has been reached. The other districts have not received this architecture rebuild.

The first progression should introduce a single driving skill at a time and then combine skills. Later stages need genuine geometry/timing variety, such as tested ramps and moving gates. Difficulty labels and gold times must be calibrated through complete human runs, contacts, corrections and completion times. Do not describe a collision-free kinematic path or teleported smoke test as a successful human playtest.

0.5.0 adds six Expert Trials with actual reverse-only control, a constrained multi-point pocket, heading-gated setup, opposite container offsets, moving lifting gates and a combined final route. They are open immediately through EXPERT TRIALS, save stars/best times separately, unlock two paints and preserve campaign unlocks. Retry reuses the scene. This addresses missing manoeuvre/timing variety, but human difficulty, gold targets and retention still need playtesting. No playable slopes/ramps were added.

The original app is installed in the reference emulator. Its rendered garage and responsive level selector have been captured. Exact original difficulty, camera/physics feel and a matched gameplay-camera comparison remain unknown until full driving runs are observed. Do not claim a parity-matched or better-than-reference build.

Emulator follow-up: original base + ARM64 split are installed. After improved host memory headroom, the Android 11 profile rendered an unobstructed garage and responded to Play/01 input. A test ad interrupted the online course load; offline loading reached a full progress bar without a verified driving screen. Emulator connectivity was restored after testing. Earlier launch/display hangs remain recorded. See [setup evidence](../apk-analysis/comparison/EMULATOR-SETUP.md). Use the captured garage frame as limited art evidence, not proof of measured difficulty or sustained gameplay.

0.6.0 upgrades common environment surfaces to 2K diffuse/normal maps plus roughness and ambient occlusion, adds screen-space contact shading and filmic grading, shaped roadside barriers, road wear/repairs and a dedicated high-speed proving ground. Six more Expert Trials combine dual timed gates, reverse-only horseshoe, parallel setups and a real 90+ km/h speed sensor. These are concrete rendering/layout improvements; the procedural buildings and flat driving surfaces remain. Human art acceptance, gold-time calibration and physical Android performance are still unmeasured. Pause > Graphics provides a Balanced mode for slower devices.
